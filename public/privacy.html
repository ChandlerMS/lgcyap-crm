Google Services Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Legacy Recruiting at crm.lgcyap.com uses Google account information when you create Gmail drafts, export applications, or enable automatic monthly application updates in Google Sheets.

Gmail drafts

When you choose Connect Gmail drafts, the CRM requests your basic Google account identity and the Gmail compose permission (gmail.compose). Google describes this permission as managing drafts and sending email. This app uses it only to create drafts you request, including your chosen recipient, subject, message, template, and template images. You review and send the draft in Gmail. The app does not request inbox-read permission or copy received messages into the CRM.

The connection must match the verified Google account signed in to the CRM. The app checks the recipient against current CRM records and sends the rendered message directly to Gmail. It does not send the message through HubSpot or log it there. Gmail holds created drafts until you send or delete them.

The CRM stores your Gmail refresh token encrypted in its server database, separately from sign-in and Sheets credentials. It stores draft request identifiers, confirmation status, and a keyed content digest to prevent duplicate creation, without storing draft message bodies in that request ledger. Your browser does not receive Google access or refresh tokens. Disconnect Gmail drafts removes this stored Gmail connection. To revoke Google permissions, use your Google Account settings; because sign-in and Sheets may use the same Google app, revocation can also affect those features. Disconnecting or revoking access does not delete existing Gmail drafts.

Google Sheets information used

When you authorize an export, the CRM requests your basic Google account identity and permission to create and manage the Google Drive files used with this feature (drive.file). The export checks your verified Google email address against the account signed in to the CRM. It then sends the selected application and review data to Google Drive to create a Google Sheet in that account.

The drive.file permission does not give this feature general access to every file in your Google Drive. It covers files created by the app or files you choose to use with the app.

Authorization and your exported file

The export requests online access and does not request a refresh token. The export implementation uses the access token to check your account and create the spreadsheet. Its temporary export-state cookie expires after ten minutes and is cleared when the export callback completes.

The resulting spreadsheet remains in your Google Drive until you delete it. You control its sharing and deletion through Google Drive. You can revoke the app's Google account connection through your Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete a spreadsheet already created or records held separately in the CRM.

Automatic monthly application and sign-in updates

The optional monthly sync is connected only by chandler@lgcyap.com. It requests offline access with the same drive.file permission so background updates can continue without an open browser. The CRM stores the refresh token encrypted in its server database, and uses it to create and update monthly application and sign-in Sheets with weekly tabs. The sign-in report includes names, phone numbers, email addresses, offices, attendance dates, sign-in answers, current recruiting stages and HubSpot links. It checks HubSpot for new applications and sign-ins every minute and refreshes them daily at 9 am Eastern. The refresh token is not sent to the browser. No additional sharing permissions are granted. Revoke access in your Google Account settings to stop the connection; previously created Sheets remain in your Drive.

Use of Google account information

Google account information is used only to provide these user-facing features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Legacy Recruiting's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
